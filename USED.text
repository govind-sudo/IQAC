Student Collection is designed as the central collection of your Student Management System. Every student has a single document that contains personal, academic, contact, family, and verification information.

Database Structure
MongoDB
│
└── iqac-student-portal
      │
      └── students   (Collection)
              │
              ├── Student Document 1
              ├── Student Document 2
              ├── Student Document 3
              └── ...

Unlike SQL databases, MongoDB stores most related information inside the student document instead of creating multiple tables.

Embedded Document Design

Your schema uses embedded documents instead of separate collections for information that belongs only to one student.

Student
│
├── father
│      ├── name
│      ├── phone
│      └── email
│
├── mother
│      ├── name
│      ├── phone
│      └── email
│
├── emergencyContact
│      ├── name
│      └── phone
│
├── presentAddress
│      ├── city
│      ├── state
│      └── pincode
│
└── permanentAddress
       ├── city
       ├── state
       └── pincode
